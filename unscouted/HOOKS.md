# HOOKS.md · Unscouted

## 1. Core message
- **Audience:** players 15–21, not in an academy, who know they have something
- **Result:** play like the pros you watch, make better decisions, get noticed
- **Pain:** no academy, no coach telling you what to fix, freezing in big moments, watching football like a fan
- **Speed:** one clip, one half, one habit you can use this weekend
- **Ease:** just your phone and the games you already watch
- **Key objection:** "I'm not in an academy, so I can't get to that level"

One line: *Unscouted helps players outside academies think and play like pros, using just the games they already watch.*

## 2. Rules for the first 3 seconds
1. **Speak to the player, not the topic.** "You", "your shots", "your team". Never open on a player name or "Today we…".
2. **One idea, under 8 words spoken.** The rest of the video earns the time.
3. **Stakes or a command.** Either something is lost ("you lose the game") or he's told to do or stop something ("Stop passing").
4. **Match the screen to the words.** Full lime flash, giant text, red on the scary word, visible on frame 1 (that's the thumbnail too).
5. **Honest.** No invented stats ("90% of players…"), no "scouts will sign you", no "every time".

## 3. Hook bank (by type)

### Pain / stakes
- Get this wrong, and you lose the game. ✅ *used: PD #1*
- This is why your coach subs you off at 70 minutes.
- You're good in training. Then the game starts and you freeze.
- One bad touch here and it's a goal for them.

### Command / contrarian
- Losing with five minutes left? Stop passing. ✅ *used: PD #2*
- Stop smashing your shots. ✅ *used: Haaland*
- Stop watching highlights. They're making you worse.
- Stop asking your coach what to work on.

### Curiosity gap (pros vs you)
- Pros make this decision before the ball even arrives. ✅ *used: PD #3*
- Watch his head, not the ball.
- Haaland's goals have one thing in common, and it isn't power.
- The best players are lazy in one specific way.

### Steal / mechanism
- Steal Vinícius's two-move trick for one-v-ones. ✅ *used: Vinícius*
- Copy this one Kane habit before your next game.
- The 3-second rule every winger should steal.

### Callout
- Not in an academy? Watch football like this.
- If you're 16 and nobody's scouted you yet, watch this.
- Wingers who always lose the ball, this one's for you.

### Challenge / quiz (Pause & Decide)
- Only real footballers get this right. A, B or C?
- You have 3 seconds. What do you do?
- Your coach is watching. Pick one.

### Transformation / story
- I failed a Barcelona academy trial at 15. Here's what they told me.
- From "you're missing the basics" to a UPSL team. What changed.
- Nobody scouted me. So I scouted the best players instead.

## 4. Top 5 (in use)
| Video | Hook | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| PD #1 | Get this wrong, and you lose the game. | Stakes in 7 words; "you" from word one |
| PD #2 | Losing with five minutes left? Stop passing. | Every player has been there; the command goes against what coaches say |
| PD #3 | Pros make this decision before the ball even arrives. | Opens a gap ("which decision?") that only the video closes |
| Haaland | Stop smashing your shots. | Command plus contrarian; hits a habit almost every young striker has |
| Vinícius | Steal Vinícius's two-move trick for one-v-ones. | Big name, specific ("two moves"), framed as a takeaway |
