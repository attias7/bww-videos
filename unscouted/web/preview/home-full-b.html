Why this exists

That’s what I was told at 15, after a trial at the Barcelona academy in Arizona. I was the best player in my street games and in every tournament we played, but my family couldn’t pay for an academy. I went to the trial as a striker, they played me at center back, and I didn’t make it.

From 15 to 21 I made sure I’d never hear that again. Gym, drills, hours against a wall. I broke down the games of more than 25 top players, analysed around 100 matches, and wrote a report on myself after every game I played. Almost 300 notes on my phone. Later I made a UPSL team after trials.

Unscouted is everything in those notes that worked, turned into a system you can follow on your own.

The whole method is one loop

Study, train, play, review. Then go again with a better question. Every week a bit better. After a year, a different player.

  1. 1

    Study

    Watch a pro. When he gets the ball, pause. See the options. Work out why he chose what he chose.

  2. 2

    Train

    Turn it into sessions. Train the 9 basics every position needs, planned with a simple points system.

  3. 3

    Play

    Use it in a real game, free in your head. Confidence, mistakes, coaches and patience.

  4. 4

    Review

    Write a match report on yourself within 24 hours. Pick the next thing to fix.
