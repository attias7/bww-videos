## Patterns
- Every page answers "what is it" + "famous 10s". Intent is split: (a) shirt meaning/history, (b) role, (c) list of players.
- Nobody teaches a player how to PLAY it: no scanning, body shape, turn vs set back, choosing the final pass, pressing as a 10.
- Zero pages have worked situations; only Cupello has one static image.
- No page explains where the 10 lives in a modern 4-3-3 (the free 8 / half-space 10) clearly with a table.
- No training plan for one player. No "how to watch a 10 on TV".
- FAQs are thin (3 questions max) and don't cover "is a 10 a midfielder or forward", "10 vs 8", "can a 10 play winger".
- Several are dated (2016) or self-contradictory.
