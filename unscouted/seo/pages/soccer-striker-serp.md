## Pattern
- Every page answers "what is a striker" and lists skills. Most list types in 1–2 lines each.
- Nobody shows HOW a striker decides: none has a single worked situation or tactics-board picture of a run, a cross, or a shoot-vs-pass moment.
- Nobody gives a training plan a player can actually follow alone; "train hard" at most.
- Nobody explains offside timing properly (no rule source), pressing triggers in concrete terms, or how to study a striker on TV.
- Several have stale "best striker right now" lists. Several have no FAQ or a thin one; none has schema-friendly answer-first blocks consistently.

## How we beat them
1. Answer-first quick box + key takeaways + question H2s with 40–60 word standalone answers (AI search).
2. Types table (6 types: poacher, target man, complete forward, pressing forward, false 9, second striker) with what they do, what to train and safe, well-known examples (no "current club" claims that go stale).
3. "How a striker decides": movement before the ball, playing on the shoulder (with IFAB Law 11 cited), near vs far post, shoot vs pass, pressing triggers.
4. 3 tactics-board situations (run in behind, far-post run, shoot vs square pass) with options, pro pick and the visual cue.
5. Weekly solo / one-friend striker plan (table), TV analysis checklist, common mistakes, 7-question FAQ.
6. Internal links to scanning, decision-making, tryouts and the other position guides; Striker Pack CTA mid + end.
