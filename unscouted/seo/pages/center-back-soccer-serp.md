## Common pattern
Definition → traits/skills list → famous players. Almost no page shows a center back's actual decisions, nobody has diagrams of real situations, nobody gives a training plan a player can do alone, nobody explains how to watch a CB on TV. FAQs are short or missing; "what number does a center back wear" and "is center back the hardest position" are answered nowhere properly.

## How we beat them
1. Answer-first definition + table of CB types (left/right CB, back 4 vs back 3, stopper, cover, ball-playing, sweeper/libero, wide CB) in one place.
2. The decisions: step vs drop rule, when to follow a dropping striker, cover/balance with partner, crosses (near post vs back post), playing out (midfield vs switch vs clear). 3 rendered tactics-board situations with options and the pro answer.
3. A weekly solo / one-friend training table, a "watch a center back on TV" checklist, common mistakes, 7-question FAQ incl. shirt numbers and hardest position. Article + FAQ schema.
4. Sources: IFAB Laws (offside, goal kick), The FA Boot Room.
