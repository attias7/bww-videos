Key takeaways

  • Most online programs train technique. Only a few train decisions, and almost none do both well.
  • Prices range from free (Anytime Soccer Training's free plan) to $399 a year (Train Effective Virtual Academy). Prices checked October 2026.
  • All are subscriptions except Unscouted, which is one-off payments.
  • An "online football academy" gives you a plan, videos and sometimes a mentor. It doesn't give you a team, matches or a contract.
  • Use the free trials before paying, and pick by your weakest area, not by the biggest feature list.

How did we pick these programs?

Every program here is one a player aged about 15 to 21 can buy and use alone, with no team or coach signing them up. Each one was checked on its official website or app store listing in October 2026, and each fact on this page links to that source.

We left out coaching courses (built for coaches), programs whose official site no longer works, and apps with no clear price or description. This compares what each program offers and costs; it isn't a months-long hands-on review. There's no 1 to 7 ranking, because the right pick depends on your weak spot.

The 7 programs, one by one

Each program gets the same checks: what it trains, what it costs, what it does better than the others, and what to watch out for. For more on decision-training apps, see best soccer IQ apps.

1. Train Effective and its Virtual Academy: best all-round program

What it is: a big training app (1M+ downloads on Google Play) with 150+ drills, workouts and courses covering technique, fitness and mentality, plus "Game Brain", where Premier League analysts break down pro players' decisions. The Virtual Academy adds a personal mentor coach, live calls and masterclasses, a weekly schedule modelled on a pro academy, an individual development plan and career planning.

Price: PRO is $13 a month billed yearly. The Virtual Academy page lists $49.99 a month, $249.99 every 6 months or $399 a year (the pricing page shows the yearly Academy plan as $33 a month). Both have a 7-day free trial.

Better than the rest at: range. It's the only program here that covers technique, fitness, mindset and some tactics, and the Academy tier is the only one with a human mentor giving you regular feedback.

Watch out for: the Academy is the most expensive option here. It mentions scouting networks but promises no trials or contracts. Game Brain shows how pros decide; it doesn't have you make the call yourself against a clock. More in our Train Effective alternative comparison.

2. Soccer IntelliGym: best for adaptive brain training

What it is: computer-based cognitive training for awareness, working memory, attention and decisions under pressure, in 30-minute sessions 2 to 3 times a week. It adjusts difficulty to you and is abstract on purpose, so you won't see real match pictures.

Better at: long, structured sessions that adapt to your level. Watch out for: no ball work and no position-specific match moments. Full breakdown: Soccer IntelliGym alternative.

3. Be Your Best: best if you own a VR headset

What it is: VR soccer training on Meta Quest for scanning, vision and decisions, with a companion phone app. Its FAQ says scenarios exist for every outfield position.

Better at: the physical habit of turning your head to scan, which a flat screen can't train. Watch out for: you need a headset, and the FAQ mentions a 12-month minimum that conflicts with the monthly plan, so ask before you pay. See IntelliGym vs Be Your Best.

4. Techne Futbol: best for ball mastery

What it is: an iOS app with weekly dribbling, passing and juggling sessions, strength and speed work, a fundamentals track for beginners, mental training sessions and a training journal. Little equipment needed.

Better at: structured technical sessions, with 4.8 stars from about 17,000 App Store ratings when we checked. Watch out for: iPhone and iPad only on the App Store, and it isn't built around decision-making.

5. Anytime Soccer Training: best low-cost option

What it is: 5,000+ follow-along videos for ball mastery, dribbling, passing, juggling, finishing and 1v1 moves, with challenges and progress tracking. A player can sign up alone.

Better at: price: the cheapest full technique library here. Watch out for: it's aimed at youth players, so some content may feel basic at 18. No decision or tactical training.

6. DribbleUp: best for guided footwork classes

What it is: a smart soccer ball that the phone app tracks while you follow live and on-demand footwork classes. The store lists 2,000+ classes and drills.

Better at: instant feedback on your touches, which a video can't give you. Watch out for: you pay for hardware and a membership, and it's marketed mostly as a gift for kids.

7. Unscouted (this one is ours): best for game IQ without a subscription

What it is: self-coaching material for players outside academies. The Basics Kit ($47) has 10 guides on analysing a player, a game, a team and yourself, a training system, a 12-week plan, worksheets and the mental side. Position packs ($17 each) cover striker, winger, number 10 and defender. Pause & Decide ($27) is an interactive quiz of 100 match situations, 20 per position, 15 seconds each, with the pro pick explained. The Game Library ($27) lists 84 pro games to analyse, with notes to compare. All one-off payments.

Better at: teaching you to read the game and coach yourself, for your position, with no monthly bill.

Watch out for: there are no drill videos, no fitness program, no live coach and no smart equipment. Pause & Decide is a fixed set of 100 situations and doesn't adapt to you. It works best next to a technique program, not instead of one. Judge it on the free test.

Is an online football academy worth it?

An online football academy is worth it if you want structure and someone checking in, and can afford around $400 a year. It gives you a weekly plan, video sessions and, at the top tier, a mentor. It can't give you teammates, real matches, a club pathway or a scout watching you play.

If you searched "football academy online" (football being what most of the world calls soccer), the main product you'll find is Train Effective's Virtual Academy. Be clear what you're buying: coaching and planning through an app and calls. You still need games at the highest level you can find, and a way to show people your football. Our guide to getting scouted without an academy covers that part.

The cheaper route is to build the same pieces yourself: a technique app, a decision trainer and a written plan you review every week.

How to choose: technique, game IQ or fitness?

Pick the program that matches your biggest weakness. If your first touch or dribbling lets you down, start with technique. If you lose the ball because you saw the pressure late or chose the wrong pass, start with game IQ. If you fade after 60 minutes, start with fitness.

FAQ

What is the best online soccer training program?

There isn't one best for everyone. Train Effective is the most complete all-round app, Techne Futbol and Anytime Soccer Training are strong for technique, and Soccer IntelliGym, Be Your Best and Unscouted focus on decision-making. Pick by your weakest area.

Is online soccer training worth it?

It can be, if you actually follow the plan and still play real games. Online training adds structured reps between team sessions. It doesn't replace matches, and no program can promise you'll get signed.

Is there a free online soccer training program?

Yes. Anytime Soccer Training has a Free Forever plan with dozens of videos. Train Effective and Soccer IntelliGym have 7-day free trials, and Unscouted's soccer IQ test gives you 8 free match situations with no sign-up.

Can I join a football academy online?

You can join an online program that calls itself an academy, like Train Effective's Virtual Academy ($49.99 a month or $399 a year, checked October 2026). It gives you a mentor, a schedule and live calls, but it isn't a club, so you still need a team to play matches.

How much does online soccer training cost?

From free to about $400 a year. Most app subscriptions sit between $10 and $40 a month, while Unscouted's products are one-off payments from $17 to $99. Prices checked October 2026.

Can online training improve soccer IQ?

Partly. Screen or VR tools let you practise reading situations and choosing an option, which you can't do with cones. Real games are still where it gets tested, so use both.
