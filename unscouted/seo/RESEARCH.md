# Unscouted: SEO keyword and competitor research

Researched 2026-10-10 with WebSearch + WebFetch only. No Semrush and no volume numbers. Keywords were picked for **buying intent**.

**Method caveat:** The WebSearch tool is not a live Google SERP, so its result order only approximates Google's. "Top results" below means the organic pages the tool returned for the exact query, excluding academic PDFs and spam or scraper domains. Before writing each page, check the live Google SERP and People Also Ask (PAA) in an incognito window. FAQ questions marked "PAA-style" are inferred from competitor FAQs and query patterns. I could not see real PAA boxes.
