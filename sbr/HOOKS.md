# HOOKS.md — Sign Build Run reels

Built with the Hormozi hook method (WHO + RESULT + SPEED/EASE + OBJECTION REMOVAL), adapted to Sign Build Run's honesty rules. The Sunday run reads this file, picks hooks from the bank (or writes new ones with the same formula), and marks each one used.

## 1. Core message
- **Audience:** introverts aged 18–28 who want their own online income but dread selling (cold calls, sales calls, DMing strangers, being on camera). They have never built a website and can spend $37, not $2,000.
- **Result:** one small local website that brings in leads a contractor pays for. The contractor pays 10% only when a job closes.
- **Pain:** every "make money online" path ends in "now get on a call".
- **Speed (honest version):** we never promise fast money. Speed lives in the *steps*: one Google search to check a town, one written post to find a contractor, one small site. First lead took about 1 to 3 months.
- **Ease:** no cold calls, no sales calls, no ad budget, no camera; you build the site with AI.
- **Key objections:** "I'm bad at sales." "I can't build a website." "It's another scam course." "I'll put in months and it won't work."

One line: *Sign Build Run helps introverts build one small lead site and get paid by a contractor, without cold calls or sales calls, honestly, not fast.*

## 2. Honesty filter (every hook must pass)
- No income, no $ earned, no "passive", "quit your job", "$10k/month", "guaranteed".
- No speed-to-money promise ("first client this week" ✗). Time numbers only from real facts: first lead ≈1–3 months; one post → 27 contractors commented.
- No fake proof. "How I" hooks may only use: 22, introvert, built it to avoid sales, first lead 1–3 months, the 27-comments post.
- Contrarian is fine, mocking people is not. Never name a guru or course.
- Under ~10 spoken words; the first 3 words must carry the callout or the tension.

## 4. Top picks (use these first)
1. **"Introverts: get contractors to pay you, no sales calls."** Calls out the exact person, names the result and removes the #1 objection in nine words. Weekly-mix slot 1 (PAIN).
2. **"Every side hustle ends with 'now get on a call.'"** Names the pain they've felt; viewer nods before you explain. Slot 1 or 7 (ENEMY).
3. **"Shy? Find a paying contractor with one written post."** Hybrid; uses the real 27-comment fact in the body. Slot 5 (talking to business owners).
4. **"Never built a site? Build one without code."** Kills the tech fear up front. Slot 2. (Body must still say ranking takes months.)
5. **"A $2,000 course won't make you less shy."** Contrarian + suspicion of guru courses, and a natural lead-in to the $37 kit. Slot 7 (max once a week).
6. **"It took me three months to get one lead. Worth it."** Honest time hook; builds trust with the suspicious viewer. Slot 3 or 6.

## 5. Usage
- **Reels / TikTok (organic):** callout, pain and hybrid hooks. Put the hook as giant text in scene 0 and say it in the first 2 seconds.

- **Email subject lines:** curiosity and how-I hooks ("How one post got twenty-seven contractors to reply").
